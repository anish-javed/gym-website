Questions

Good to know

Can men and women train together?

From 5 PM to 1 AM the gym is open to everyone, men and women. From 10 AM to 4 PM it is for ladies only.

Do you have lockers?

Yes, lockers are available so you can keep your things safe while you train.

Can I choose a female or male trainer?

Yes. When you join, you tell us if you prefer a female or a male trainer. We have both.

How much does it cost?

Admission is Rs 2,000 (one time) and membership is Rs 5,000 per month. Use the calculator on the Membership page to see your total.

How do I pay?

After you send the join form, you pay with USDT on the Solana network. The Membership page shows the wallet address, a QR code and the exact amount, step by step.

What should I bring on my first day?

Bring comfortable clothes, sports shoes, a small towel and a water bottle. We will take care of the rest.

Ready to start?

It takes about two minutes to join.
